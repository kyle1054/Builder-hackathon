01 — The idea

It's about the journey, not the destination.

Every navigation app optimises for arrival time, so the country you drive through becomes scenery behind glass. SideQuest optimises for the opposite: the main quest is getting there, the side quests are everything worth stopping for on the way. The mark is that sentence drawn — a road running to the horizon, and the thing off to the side you'd have missed.

03 — Wordmark & lockups

Letterforms are a 5×7 bitmap grid — the wordmark is pixel art, which is why the rest of the system doesn't need to be. SIDE in parchment, QUEST in gold: the detour is the part that lights up. Clear space on all sides = the height of one letter. Never below 16px. Never recoloured outside the palette.

04 — Palette · night drive, warm dashboard light

Foreground white is parchment #F5F1E8, never pure #FFFFFF — that single choice carries most of the warmth. Gold is the only CTA colour; never place two golds in competition on one screen. Dark is the only mode.

System sans throughout (SF Pro / Roboto): it renders instantly, respects the user's accessibility text size, and keeps the pixel accents feeling deliberate rather than like one more novelty typeface.

06 — The 20% pixel rule

Pixel art is a texture, not a theme.

It carries personality at roughly one-fifth of the screen. The contrast between crisp, modern chrome and chunky pixel accents is the look — go past it and you lose both the charm and the legibility.

20% PIXEL
80% CLEAN, LEGIBLE UI
✓ Pixels live here Quest & place icons · badges and XP rewards · the vehicle marker on the route · journal stamps and frames · empty states · arrival celebrations
✕ Never here Body text · form fields · buttons · navigation chrome · the map itself · anything on the Pilot's screen · anything under 24px
07 — Voice

A friend who knows the area and thinks you should pull over.

Warm, curious, slightly wry. Short sentences, second person. Real place names do the work — we never invent lore, and we never narrate like a dungeon master.

Say this“There's an orchard 6 minutes off the road.”
Not this“A hidden orchard appears on the quest radar.”
Say this“You stopped for the pie. Correct decision.”
Not this“Tavern rest complete. Well Rested buff gained.”
Say this“Worth the stop. Trust us.”
Not this“Mountain mist reveals an old traveler's riddle.”
